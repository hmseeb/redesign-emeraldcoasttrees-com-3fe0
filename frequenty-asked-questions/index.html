Good to know

Your tree service questions, answered

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, we are fully licensed and insured to provide tree services across the Emerald Coast. Our team includes professional arborists committed to delivering safe and professional tree care.

Do you offer free estimates?

Absolutely! We provide free, no-obligation estimates for all our services. Contact us to schedule an on-site assessment.

When is tree removal necessary?

Tree removal may be necessary if a tree is dead, diseased, structurally unsound, or poses a safety hazard. Our experts assess each situation to determine the best course of action.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree?

Permit requirements vary by municipality. For example, in Fort Walton Beach, a permit may be required depending on the tree’s size and type. We can assist you in navigating local regulations.

Will you remove the stump after tree removal?

Stump removal is a separate service. We offer professional stump grinding to eliminate unsightly stumps and prevent regrowth.

Do you offer tree planting services?

Yes, we provide expert tree planting services, including species selection, proper planting techniques, and guidance on care to ensure healthy growth.

Is stump grinding necessary?

While not mandatory, stump grinding prevents potential hazards, pest infestations, and regrowth, enhancing the safety and appearance of your property.

Do you provide emergency tree services?

Yes, we offer 24/7 emergency tree services for storm damage, fallen trees, and other urgent situations to ensure your property’s safety.
