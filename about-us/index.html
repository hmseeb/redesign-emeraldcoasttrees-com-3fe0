About Us

A family-owned Emerald Coast tree care company built on hard work, integrity and a genuine commitment to the community we serve.

Get to know us

Honesty, integrity & experience

At All About Trees On The Emerald Coast, tree care is more than just our job — it’s our passion. As a family-owned and operated business, we’ve built our reputation on hard work, integrity, and a genuine commitment to the people and properties we serve across Northwest Florida. With many years of hands-on experience, our team brings deep-rooted knowledge of proper tree care, safe removal techniques, and expert trimming that helps protect and beautify your landscape. Whether it’s a small residential job or a large commercial project, we treat every property like it’s our own.

What truly sets us apart is our dedication to giving back to the community that supports us. We believe in building lasting relationships — not just with trees, but with people. From sponsoring local events to providing discounts for military families and seniors, we’re proud to support the place we call home. At All About Trees On The Emerald Coast, we combine experience, personalized service, and a love for our community to deliver tree care you can trust. Let us take care of your trees — the right way.
